About

Wizards are listed in earthly registers under various names. This is a courtesy on their part. They are, after all, independent spirits, and attempts to confine them in definitions usually end with the definition going missing.

Each of them has a magic of their own. Out of a few vibrations of the air, they can make a space larger than the room they sit in. They sense that reality does not end where our ways of knowing it end.

That is why they create. They send sounds, images and words into the darkness, guided by the peculiar conviction that freedom can be heard.

Frank Myonk works with artist Malga Kubiak on the book One Man Show, the film Fetish and the performance Mango Lassi.
